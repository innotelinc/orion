Your files, on your own machine.

Orion is a desktop for one server: the files on its data volume, its spaces and shares, the apps it has installed and the systemd units that serve them. Everything you see is read from the machine, and every change is written to it.

Files that are really there.

Every file action is a call on the machine's data volume, not a picture of one. Trash is recoverable, and a delete is a delete.

Apps that are really installed.

Sharing without a second password.
